TV Movie Tracker is a watch-list app for TV shows and movies.

* Track watching, finished and history lists for shows and films
* New-episode notifications
* Discover trending shows and movies
* Personal ratings, statistics and a calendar of air dates
* Home-screen widget with your next unwatched episodes
* Backup and restore your library; no account needed

TV data comes from TVMaze (no key required). Movie data and where-to-watch info via TMDB, which needs a free key entered in the app's Settings.
